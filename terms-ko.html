Delivery. Upon arrival, the letter becomes visible to the recipient. The recipient may keep the letter in their local letterbox. You cannot unilaterally revoke content already delivered.

Account Deletion. Upon account deletion, undeparted letters are terminated; departed letters continue to their destination as scheduled. Account deletion is permanent and irreversible.

Connection Removal. If a connection is severed, already received letters remain preserved in respective mailboxes, while undeparted letters follow recall rules.
