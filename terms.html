2. What the App Does

The App lets you send letters to people you know in real life. Virtual couriers carry letters between the cities you choose. The App estimates delivery time from the distance between city reference points and the selected courier and transport settings. Routes shown on the map illustrate the journey; they are not GPS tracking or real-world navigation.

The App exclusively serves communications between established acquaintances:

  • No stranger matching, no public community, no discovery feeds, and no algorithmic content recommendations;

  • Recipients are specifically designated by you, and recipients must also use the App to receive letters;

  • No mass messaging, broadcast channels, or public dissemination.

These limitations are integral to our product, and we will not transform this into a social network for connecting strangers in future releases.

6. What Happens After a Letter Is Sent

Recall. You may recall a letter free of charge while it remains eligible for recall in the App. A letter that has departed may still be recalled only before delivery and while it is outside the final 20 minutes before its expected delivery time. A successful recall ends that delivery; stamps or timing services already used for it are not refunded.

Delivery. Arrival in the recipient’s city is not always delivery. A scheduled letter that arrives early remains sealed until its chosen delivery time. Once the letter is made available, the recipient can open and save it. You cannot recall a letter after it has been delivered.

Account Deletion. Deleting your account cancels sends that have not departed. Letters that have departed—including scheduled letters already waiting in the recipient’s city—continue under the delivery plan already set. Deleting your App account does not cancel an Apple-billed membership; manage or cancel it in your Apple account subscription settings. After deletion, you cannot manage those letters or use the deleted account again.

Monthly Membership. While your membership is active, you can use current and future items of the same kinds—stamps, stationery, decorations, and timing services—without consuming your existing paid inventories. New coin top-ups and item purchases are unavailable during membership; existing coins, stamps, and timing-service units remain in your account. When membership expires, membership-only use rights end for new letters. Unused paid stamps and timing-service units remain available, and letters already departed keep their existing delivery commitments and timing benefits.
